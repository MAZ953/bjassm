About us

Our Journey Since 1979

A commitment to expanding educational opportunities and supporting social development in rural Gujarat.

Bharuch Jilla Adarsh Samaj Seva Mandal was established with a commitment to expanding educational opportunities and supporting social development in rural Gujarat.

Over the years the organization has developed a network of schools, colleges, residential Ashramshalas, hostels and educational programmes serving communities in and around Narmada district. Our institutions aim to reduce barriers to education by bringing learning closer to rural and tribal communities.

Today our work extends beyond formal education into professional learning, nursing, vocational skills, environmental awareness, student welfare and community development.

Our Vision

To build an educated, empowered, skilled and self-reliant society in which every individual, particularly those from rural and tribal communities, has opportunities to learn, grow and improve their quality of life.

Our Mission

  • Promote accessible, quality education at different academic levels
  • Support tribal children through residential education and welfare
  • Strengthen higher, professional and nursing education
  • Expand vocational training and employment-oriented skills
  • Encourage women's education and youth empowerment
  • Promote environment, health awareness and community service
  • Build partnerships that improve education and livelihoods

Our activities

Learning Beyond the Classroom

We believe education should develop responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

22 July 2025

Tree Plantation & Best Out of Waste

Students and staff of B.Ed., B.R.S. and M.S.W. Colleges planted trees at Mangrol, with discussions on environmental protection and a Best Out of Waste competition.

17 July 2025

Student Cultural Competitions

Mehndi, Rangoli, hairstyling, saree draping and other creative competitions encouraging expression and confidence.

1 August 2025

Nari Vandan Utsav

Jointly organized with the District Women and Child Officer's Office and Abhayam 181 Women Helpline: Beti Bachao awareness, prevention of female foeticide and a village rally.

August 2025

World Tribal Day & Raksha Bandhan

Celebrating tribal heritage, traditional songs, dances and cultural identity.

15 August 2025

Independence Day

Flag hoisting, patriotic addresses and student performances at Mangrol College Campus with students, teachers, trustees and the local community.

5 September 2025

Teachers' Day

Students and faculty recognised the importance of teachers in education and personal development.

2 October 2025

Gandhi Jayanti & Cleanliness Awareness

Ram Dhun, student engagement, environmental awareness and cleanliness activities in the campus and village.

18 November 2025

Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan

A pledge programme supporting a drug-free India and responsible social behaviour.

4–5 December 2025

Uttarvahini Narmada Cleanliness Drive

Students and staff cleaned areas near the Ranchhodraiji Temple at Rampura and Sitaram Ashram along the Parikrama route, with discussion on the significance of the Narmada River.

1 October 2026

Van Mahotsav

Shri Narmada College of Education, Mangrol, with the Social Forestry Range, Rajpipla: tree plantation and awareness with public representatives, forestry officials, educators and students.

🇮🇳

National Service & Institutional Support

BJAASM has provided temporary accommodation and campus facilities for personnel of the Border Security Force (BSF) and Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) during preparations for ceremonial events at the Statue of Unity, Ekta Nagar, reported at approximately 15 days annually over the past two years. This reflects our commitment to community cooperation and public service.
